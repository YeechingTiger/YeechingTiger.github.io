I work half in operations and half in research. As Faculty Lead for Strategic Data Science Infrastructure at Regenstrief Data Services, I help run the data and NLP services that researchers and health systems in Indiana use every day. As an assistant professor in the Department of Biostatistics and Health Data Science at the IU School of Medicine, I study the problems those services keep running into.

The pattern is the same each time: find where real clinical data work gets stuck, pick the research method that actually fits, and put the result back into the service so the next request is faster and more trustworthy.

Recruiting. Postdocs, PhD students, research engineers, and interns. Details below.

What I work on

Everything below runs on the Indiana Network for Patient Care, one of the largest health information exchanges in the US.

Reading the chart at scale

Stuck
Many studies need facts that live only in clinical notes. Today that means manual chart review: slow, costly, and hard to audit.
Method
LLM agents that read longitudinal notes like an expert abstractor, cite the evidence behind each answer, and are evaluated against expert review so a correct answer can be told from a lucky one.
Back
Shapes the NLP-as-a-Service and note de-identification offerings I lead at Regenstrief Data Services.

Lung-RADS with LLMs · LLMs for medical coding · Auditable chart review (code)

Finding the right patients for a trial

Stuck
Screening patients for clinical trials is mostly manual, one trial at a time, and eligibility criteria are written for people, not computers.
Method
LLMs that turn free-text criteria into computable rules, structured screening first and notes second to keep cost down, and ontology-aware retrieval for cohort discovery.
Back
A trial-matching pipeline we are building with IU Health for trial recruitment across Indiana.

Eligibility criteria parsing · LLMs for trial matching

Making real-world data fit for evidence

Stuck
EHR and claims data were not built for research. Every study cleans them again, and few check whether the data can answer the question at all.
Method
OMOP pipelines, validated computable phenotypes, data-quality checks, and target trial emulation for drug safety and effectiveness.
Back
Shared INPC-OMOP data and reusable phenotypes at Regenstrief, used by studies in Alzheimer's disease, multiple sclerosis, and cancer.

Medicare claims to OMOP · Data quality for TTE · Alzheimer's disease phenotype
