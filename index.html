Xing He

Assistant Professor · Indiana University School of Medicine

I am an assistant professor in the Department of Biostatistics and Health Data Science at the Indiana University School of Medicine and a research scientist at the Regenstrief Institute, where I am also Faculty Lead for Strategic Data Science Infrastructure at Regenstrief Data Services. I hold an adjunct appointment in Purdue's Weldon School of Biomedical Engineering and am a member of the IU Simon Comprehensive Cancer Center. I received my Ph.D. in biomedical informatics and M.S. in computer science from the University of Florida, where I later served as Research Assistant Professor and Assistant Director of Research Infrastructure at UF Health.

I work half in operations and half in research: I help run the data and NLP services that researchers and health systems in Indiana use, study the problems those services keep running into, and put the results back into practice.

Research

I work where clinical data operations meet research. My work moves from clinical NLP to large language models to agentic AI, and asks at every step whether the answer can be trusted enough to use in research and care. It falls into five groups:

1. Reading the clinical record: NLP, LLMs, and agentic chart review

Extracting reliable facts from clinical notes, radiology reports, and images, and building agents that review a patient's record like an expert abstractor and cite verifiable evidence for each answer.

Papers: Family history extraction · Cognitive tests in notes · Pulmonary nodules · Lung-RADS with LLMs · Image to report · Medical coding · Code

Projects: agentic cancer chart review and guideline concordance; an agentic EHR audit system with note de-identification; provenance-based evaluation of medical LLM agents; LLM extraction of pain outcomes, radiology findings, and immunotherapy adverse events; agent-assisted measurement of patient–clinician communication.

2. Clinical trials: eligibility, matching, and design

Making eligibility criteria computable, matching patients to trials across a statewide network, and using real-world data to design more representative trials.

Papers: Criteria parsing with LLMs · LLMs for trial matching · Temporal logic for trials · Eligibility criteria and participant age (AMIA 2022) · Ontology-driven eligibility (AMIA 2018)

Projects: an LLM trial-matching pipeline with IU Health; ontology-aware retrieval for cohort discovery; an Alzheimer's disease trial simulation system; the Patient-Trial Matching Challenge @ IEEE ICHI 2027.

3. Causal evidence from real-world data

Target trial emulation, drug safety and effectiveness, and multi-site and federated analysis on EHR and claims data, including agents that help design emulations.

Papers: Causal inference in ML · Data quality for TTE · GLP-1 RA effectiveness · Temporal deconfounder · Federated GLMs · AGENT-TTE (KDD AEGIS 2026)

Projects: safety and effectiveness of multiple sclerosis therapies; GLP-1 receptor agonists in cancer survivors and aging; vaccine and drug-safety target trial emulations; agentic target trial emulation; federated multi-site studies.

4. Data and knowledge foundations

Validated computable phenotypes, OMOP harmonization, ontologies, and knowledge graphs that make models and agents reproducible across sites.

Papers: Alzheimer's disease phenotype · Glioblastoma phenotype · Alcohol-use disorder phenotype · Medicare claims to OMOP · Semantic integration review · Knowledge graph visualization

Projects: a computable phenotype library and agents that author concept sets; LLM-enhanced phenotypes; laboratory data quality; agentic ontology and knowledge-graph engineering; semantic standards and tools for exposome data.

5. AI in clinical workflows and population health

Bringing predictive models into care: risk scores and decision support in the EHR, social determinants of health, and digital health tools.

Papers: Risk scores in EHR workflows · Fair polysocial risk score · Social-risk decision support · Lung cancer screening · Asthma mHealth app

Projects: social risk management for patients with dementia; lung cancer screening in breast cancer survivors; a social determinants of health atlas; a guideline-adherence agent for asthma care.
