Group

IU School of Medicine · Regenstrief Institute

We build trustworthy agentic AI for healthcare: AI agents that do real clinical data work and can be audited, evaluated, and deployed. Our problems come from the data and NLP services at Regenstrief Data Services and from clinicians at IU Health: reading clinical notes at scale, matching patients to trials, and making EHR and claims data fit for evidence. What we build goes back into those services.

Join us

The problems here have real users waiting on the answer. You will work next to the people who run the data services and the clinicians who use them, on large-scale EHR data in secure computing environments, and you will write first-author papers about what you build. Strong candidates usually come from biomedical informatics, computer science, data science, or statistics.

Postdoctoral fellows

Clinical NLP and LLMs: agentic chart review, multimodal models over notes and structured EHR, trial matching, and evaluation against expert review.

PhD students

Informatics and health AI. Please email me before you apply; I will help you find the IU program that fits your background.

Research engineers and data scientists

For people who like building things that stay running: OMOP and INPC pipelines, LLM services, and evaluation tooling.

Interns and visiting students

Undergraduate, master's, or visiting students, for a well-scoped project over a semester or summer.

To apply, email hexing@iu.edu with the subject “[Join] Position – Your Name”, your CV, a few sentences on what you want to work on, and one paper or code sample you are proud of. Applications are reviewed on a rolling basis.
